## Multiple Practices: target design (#13)

- **Records.** Each item is held by the Practice that recorded it. A **Combined View** shows the Patient across Practices; items are read-only outside their Holding Practice, and conflicts are shown side by side, never merged.
- **Access Grants.** Given by a clinician at the Holding Practice, backed by the Patient's **written, signed Sharing Consent** (uploaded and logged). Each grant is scoped (full record / summary / selected Documents), read-only, expires after 12 months by default, and can be revoked. Every view is logged. Receiving Practices can request a grant but not give one.
- **Still open:** how to confirm two Practices' records are the same person (Medicare + DOB + name? manual confirmation?), and whether the Patient is notified of access.
